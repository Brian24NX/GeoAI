Do the chips show the trail?

Each picture is a 96 m × 96 m aerial photo (NAIP) centred on a spot on one of our trails, the same view the image model sees in Track B. The red line is where the matched GPS tracks put the trail. For each one, pick:

  • Visible, on the line: you can see a path, clearing or bare strip under the red line.
  • Visible, off the line: you can see the trail, but clearly beside the red line.
  • Hidden: trees or shadow cover it, so the photo can't show the trail itself.
  • Can't tell.

Each click is saved to a file on the Mac, so closing or reloading this page loses nothing. When you're done, tell Claude "done".
