1. Implementation details are in Methods

You asked whether the model is an MLP or an encoder–decoder. There is no encoder–decoder. Most models are fitted curves, ridge regression and boosted trees. The one neural network is the learned Tobler (Table I, last row): a small MLP.

  • Network: 12 terrain inputs → two hidden layers of 32 (SiLU) → 5 outputs: each segment's own curve constants and a spread. About 1.9k weights.
  • Training: Adam, learning rate 0.001, batches of 2,048, Student-t loss, early stopping, 3 seeds.
  • Other models: robust least squares for the curves; the ridge penalty is picked on held-out hikes; trees use learning rate 0.05, up to 1,000 rounds and 31 leaves. Pretrained embeddings are frozen.

The paper still fits in 4 pages, not counting references.
