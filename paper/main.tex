\begin{abstract}
\todo{150--200 words, written last. Problem: travel time on foot; Tobler.
Data: 17 regions, 290k walking segments from public OSM GPS traces. Test:
leave one region out, calibrated predictive distributions (CRPS). Result:
terrain from airborne-lidar elevation transfers to unseen regions (+2.1 to
+2.8\% CRPS skill over a refitted slope curve), map tags barely do (+0.4 to
+0.9\%), satellite embeddings help within regions but transfer less
reliably; Tobler is 12.5\% worse. A terrain-adjusted hiking function needs
one DEM-derived input.}
\end{abstract}
