\todo{Sources: OSM GPS traces~\cite{osm} from the trackpoints API (5,000
points a page), the original files of named uploads, and a lab crawl of
1.5M original files (2016--2022). 17 regions in the US and Canada (Fig.~1);
16 from full downloads, the Bay Area from the crawl only.}

\todo{Pipeline in one paragraph: duplicates and group hikes merged into
outings; stops removed (60~s / 10~m window); hidden-Markov map matching to
OSM trails~\cite{newson2009hidden} (97\% of points matched); 50~m segments
along the matched path; slope from USGS 3DEP~\cite{sugarbaker2017status};
travel mode anchored on foot-only trails, checked against 327 traces whose
own app named the activity (5\% of ``walkers'' labeled bike or ski).}

\todo{Numbers: 2,876 hiking traces; the walking analysis set has 2,046
traces in 1,992 outings, 4,702 moving hours, 290,640 segments (14,532~km). 1~m lidar under at least 83\%
of walked trail in 13 regions.}
