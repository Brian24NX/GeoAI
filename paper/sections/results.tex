\todo{R1: Tobler fails everywhere (Table~\ref{tab:skill}): $-12.5\%$, peak
4.31~km/h at $-4.5\%$ grade, not 6. Campbell's published curve ties our
refit on average but is region-dependent ($-10\%$ in the Adirondacks).}

\todo{R2: tags do not transfer: $+1.3\%$ within regions, $+0.9\%$ / $+0.4\%$
in unseen ones.}

\todo{R3: terrain transfers: $+2.1\%$ / $+2.8\%$ in unseen regions, beating
tags in 16 of 17 (Fig.~\ref{fig:regions}); terrain minus tags $+0.7$ to
$+2.2$, above zero in every bootstrap draw. About 60\% of the gain is
uneven grade inside the segment. Not a region proxy (adds $\sim$1.9 points
over region offsets), not a hiker proxy. 1~m lidar $\approx$ 5~m; STRIDE's
roughness adds nothing on trails; 30~m vegetation adds nothing; embeddings
help within regions but transfer less reliably.}

\todo{R4: the hiker dominates whole-hike time (Fig.~\ref{fig:hike}):
median error 13.6\% (slope) $\to$ 13.2\% (terrain) $\to$ 9.5\% (plus the
hiker's first km).}
